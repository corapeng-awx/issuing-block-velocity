What happened and what we are doing

Three velocity rules designed to detect card testing blocked legitimate corporate travel bookings. The rapid-fire pattern (transactions 1-2 seconds apart) mimics card testing but is normal for travel platforms processing flight + hotel pairs.

A dedicated issuing DA joined last month and has already revamped several rules, lifting precision 2x (8.4% to 17.8%, August backtest at 24%). Shadow rule launched last week to validate on live data. Overall issuing fraud rate is 0.03% against a 0.2% target, giving us room to relax thresholds and reduce friction.
